Title: Edmund Sturton
Length: 565 characters

============================================================

edmund sturton (or stourton, fl. late 15th-early 16th century) was an english composer of the tudor period. nothing is known about his life and career, but he is believed to be the same sturton whose six-part setting of ave maria ancilla trinitatis is found in the lambeth choirbook. another six-part setting, gaude virgo mater christi, is in the eton choirbook; its voices cover a range of fifteen notes. both compositions are attributed merely to 'stourton' or 'sturton', but in the eton choirbook a later had has added the christian name 'edmundus'.
