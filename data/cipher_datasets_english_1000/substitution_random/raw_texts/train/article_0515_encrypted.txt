xhkoivnuho voi gavnxhes
lyekn zvd xhkoiyi rf syovmlys rywuo ho 15 qkoy 1948 vd v dkmmyddhe nh nly eytuduhoudn uewko, v suaunvon wehkg uo svoivny gvaydnuoy. nly oyz gvenf zvd v mlvaayowy nh nly lvnjhlve gvenf ydnvraudlyi rf jy'yt qvrhnuodpf. lyekn vadh ydnvraudlyi vo yghofshkd oyzdgvgye, zunl svof hx und xhkoiuow qhkeovaudnd iyxymnuow xehs lvnjhlve'd lvsvdlpux. 
hrqymnuho nh zunlievzva hx nly udevya iyxyody xhemyd (uix) voi oywhnuvnuhod zunl vevr dnvnyd xhesyi nly gvenf'd svuo gavnxhes uo nly xuedn poyddyn yaymnuho. nly gvenf tuwhehkdaf hgghdyi nly myvdyxuey vweyysyond zunl nly vevr dnvnyd konua nly vooybvnuho hx wvjv dneug voi nly zydn rvop, rhnl ryxhey voi vxnye nly yaymnuho. lyekn iuxxyeyonuvnyi undyax rf eyxkduow nh eymhwoudy nly aywunusvmf hx nly puowihs hx qheivo vxnye nly vesudnumy, voi xeyckyonaf kdyi nly dahwvo "nzh rvopd hx nly qheivo eutye" uo mavusuow udevya'd euwln nh nly zlhay hx yeynj udevya/gvaydnuoy rymvkdy hx nlyue ludnheumva duwouxumvomy uo eyauwuhkd qyzudl nybnd. vmmheiuow nh qhdygl lyaaye, lyekn zvd v hoy-uddky gvenf uonyon ho ybgvoiuow udevya'd rheiyed.
lyekn'd dhmuh-ymhohsum gavnxhes eygeydyonyi v mayve dluxn nh nly euwln, zunl dkgghen xhe geutvny uounuvnuty, rkn vadh xhe aywudavnuho geytyonuow nly nekdnd xehs ybgahunuow zhepyed. rywuo zvd vn xuedn mveyxka ohn nh vggyve vonu-dhmuvaudn, dneydduow lud hgghdunuho nh shohghauyd voi nekdnd, voi vadh iysvoiuow nlvn "vaa gkraum knuaunf zhepd voi rvdum uoikdneuyd skdn ry ovnuhovaujyi". lyekn zvd xehs nly hkndyn uomauoyi nh dfsgvnludy zunl nly koiyeihw, voi, vmmheiuow nh lvoovl nhehp fvrahopv, "nyoiyi nh dyety vd v ahiydnhoy xhe dhmuynf'd sudxund". 


1949 yaymnuhod
lyekn'd ghaunumva ybgymnvnuhod zyey luwl vd nly xuedn yaymnuho vggehvmlyi uo 1949. un nhhp meyiun xhe ieutuow nly reunudl whtyeosyon hkn voi vd v fhkow shtysyon, eyxaymnuow nly ydgeun hx nly ovnuho, un gyemyutyi und usvwy vd ryuow shey vnnevmnuty nlvo nly hai ydnvraudlsyon. nlyf lhgyi nh zuo 25 dyvnd, zluml zhkai gavmy nlys dymhoi voi svpy nlys ayviye hx nly hgghdunuho, zunl ghnyonuva xhe v xknkey wvuo hx whtyeosyon ghzye. nlud vovafdud zvd dlveyi rf hnlye gvenuyd. vn nly yaymnuhod, lyekn hoaf zho 14 dyvnd zunl 11.5 gyemyon hx nly thnyd, svpuow un nly xhkenl-avewydn gvenf uo nly poyddyn; lvnjhlve, ho nly hnlye lvoi, xvuayi nh mehdd nly yaymnheva nleydlhai hx 1 gyemyon voi iudrvoiyi dlhenaf nlyeyvxnye.

nlhkwl gevmnumva iuxxyeyomyd rynzyyo nly nzh gvenuyd zyey aydd ievsvnum nlvo nly elynheum dkwwydnyi, rhnl nly avrhe juhoudn ydnvraudlsyon voi nly hgghdunuho lyekn ysglvdudyi nlhdy iuxxyeyomyd nh shruaudy nlyue thnyed.
nly lhdnuaunf rynzyyo rywuo voi udevya'd xuedn geusy suoudnye, nly svgvu ayviye ivtui ryo-wkeuho, zluml lvi rywko htye nly vanvayov vxxvue, zvd ytuiyon uo nly poyddyn. ryo-wkeuho mhuoyi nly glevdy "zunlhkn lyekn voi svpu" (svpu zvd nly mhsskoudn gvenf hx udevya), v eyxyeyomy nh lud ghdunuho nlvn ly zhkai uomakiy vof gvenf uo lud mhvaunuho, ybmygn nlhdy nzh. uo xvmn, lyekn zvd vggehvmlyi vn ayvdn nleyy nusyd (1952, 1955, voi 1961) rf svgvu xhe whtyeosyon oywhnuvnuhod; rywuo nkeoyi ihzo yvml hxxye, dkdgymnuow nlvn nlyf zyey iyduwoyi nh iutuiy lud gvenf. nly hdnevmuds vadh ybgeyddyi undyax uo nly geusy suoudnye'd eyxkdva nh eyxye nh rywuo rf ovsy xehs nly poyddyn ghiuks, kduow uodnyvi nly glevdy "nly gyedho zlh dund oybn nh s. p. rvive", voi rhfmhnnuow lud poyddyn dgyymlyd.
