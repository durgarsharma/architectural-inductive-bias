Title: Orkhan Ibadov
Length: 548 characters

============================================================

orkhan ibadov (azerbaijani: orxan elxan oğlu i̇badov, ukrainian: орхан ельханович ібадов; born 20 june 1998) is a ukrainian football defender of azerbaijani origin who plays for sport london e benfica.


career
ibadov is a product of the different ukrainian youth sportive schools.
in september 2017 he signed a contract with fc arsenal kyiv in the ukrainian first league.
during the 2024–25 season, ibadov signed for english club sport london e benfica.
