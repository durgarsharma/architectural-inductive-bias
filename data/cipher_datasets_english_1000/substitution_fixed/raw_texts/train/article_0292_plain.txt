Title: Charles Rodwell
Length: 1153 characters

============================================================

charles rodwell (born 26 june 1996) is a french politician of agir who has been serving as a member of the national assembly since 2022, representing yvelines's 1st constituency.


early life
rodwell was born in london, england to a british father and french mother.


political career
rodwell was elected to the national assembly in yvelines's 1st constituency in the 2022 french legislative election; at the time, he was – alongside louis margueritte and charles sitzenstuhl – one of three former staff members of bruno le maire who moved from the ministry of economics and finance to parliament.
in parliament, rodwell has since been serving on the committee on economic affairs. in this capacity, he served as the parliament's rapporteur on measures aimed at attracting foreign businesses to expand in france in 2023.
in addition to his committee assignments, rodwell is part of the french parliamentary friendship groups with côte d'ivoire, cameroon, and ethiopia.


political positions
rodwell is considered to be part of his parliamentary group's conservative wing.
