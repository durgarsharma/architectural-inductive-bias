Title: List of American exchange-traded funds
Length: 5000 characters

============================================================

this is a table of notable american exchange-traded funds, or etfs. as of 2020, the number of exchange-traded funds worldwide was over 7,600, representing about 7.74 trillion u.s. dollars in assets. the largest etf, as of april 2021, was the spdr s&p 500 etf trust (nyse arca: spy), with about $353.4 billion in assets.  the second-largest was the ishares core s&p 500 etf with around $270.0 billion (nyse arca: ivv), and third-largest was the vanguard total stock market etf (nyse arca: vti) with $213.1 billion.
