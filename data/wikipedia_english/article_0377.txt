Title: Bohunice, Ilava District

Bohunice (Hungarian: Vágbanya) is a village and municipality in Ilava District in the Trenčín Region of north-western Slovakia.


History
In historical records the village was first mentioned in 1229.


Geography
The municipality lies at an altitude of 276 metres and covers an area of 7.036 km². It has a population of about 752 people.


Genealogical resources
The records for genealogical research are available at the state archive "Statny Archiv in Bratislava, Slovakia"

Roman Catholic church records (births/marriages/deaths): 1768-1896 (parish A)
