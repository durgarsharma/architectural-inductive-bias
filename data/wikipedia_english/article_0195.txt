Title: 2026 Texas Senate election

Elections to the Texas Senate will be held concurrently to the 2026 Texas House of Representatives elections on November 3, 2026.


Retirements
District 3: Robert Nichols is retiring
District 4: Brandon Creighton resigned early to serve as Chancellor of the Texas Tech University System
District 11: Mayes Middleton is retiring to run for Attorney General
District 22: Brian Birdwell is retiring


Special elections
As of July 2025, one special election is scheduled to fill an unexpired term.


2025 District 9 special election
Kelly Hancock resigned to become chief clerk of the Texas Comptroller's office in June 2025. A special election is scheduled to be held on November 4th, 2025.
